## Short description

Nexus is a bilingual, interactive Marvel audiovisual tracker. It helps fans
follow movies, series, seasons, episodes, alternate universes, and narrative
connections; create and share watch marathons; unlock achievements; and compare
progress with friends without losing control of spoilers.
